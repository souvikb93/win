FAQ

Good to know.

Is WIN really free for producers?

Yes. Producers enter and manage their product data at no cost. Merchants and wholesalers pay for the data they request.

Does WIN work with our ERP?

WIN connects to ERP systems so requests and data travel machine to machine. If you aren't connected yet, you can use the WIN web app straight away. We'll check your system in the demo.

Where is our data stored?

On servers in Germany, under German and EU data protection law. WIN isn't an open pool: only merchants who send a request receive a producer's data.

Which data can WIN handle?

More than 200 attributes per wine, including descriptions, aromas, media, LMIV labelling, logistics and packaging data for LUCID reports. Merchants decide which fields are required.

Can WIN connect us to GDSN?

Yes. The optional EuvinoPRO GDSN module publishes and receives product data in a GDSN data pool, sent straight from your ERP via API.
