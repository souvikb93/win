Frequently Asked Questions About WIN

Find answers to common questions about WIN and how it supports the beverage industry.

What is WIN?

WIN is EuvinoPRO's beverage data management platform, helping businesses exchange structured product information digitally.

Who is WIN designed for?

WIN supports beverage producers, retailers, distributors, and trade agencies.

What product information can be exchanged?

Depending on the workflow, this can include product names, barcodes, packaging details, origin, and other product attributes.

Can WIN work with our existing business systems?

WIN supports connections with existing business systems. Contact the team to discuss your organisation's requirements.

How can we learn more about WIN?

Send us a message or use the Book a Demo option to discuss how WIN could support your business.
