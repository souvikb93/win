WeinWirtschaft · 11 March 2019 · Partnership

Hawesko bets on WIN

Original title: “Hawesko setzt auf WIN”

The Hanseatisches Wein- und Sekt-Kontor becomes the first major WIN partner. Hawesko and its subsidiaries handle data on six-figure numbers of wines and producers every year. With WIN they want to fix missing and wrong details like producer address, alcohol content or sulphites at the source, together with the producers.

  • Up to 170attributes per wine at the time
  • Freefor producers
  • Pay per requestfor wholesale, gastronomy and food retail

About WIN

WIN (Wein- und Getränke-Informations-Netzwerk) is the product-data network of EuvinoPRO, Euvino GmbH, Berlin. It connects producers, trade agencies, merchants and distributors in the beverage industry and automates the exchange of validated product master data, machine to machine.
