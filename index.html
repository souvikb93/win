Trusted by leading wine merchants, importers and retailers

The problem

Master data shouldn't arrive as a PDF.

Wine and beverage data is complex: vintages, allergens, alcohol, logistics, packaging. Most of it still travels by email attachment and gets typed in by hand.

Without WIN

Every new article starts a chase.

  • Excel lists and PDF datasheets from every supplier, each in its own format
  • Retyping data into the ERP, field by field
  • Manual GTIN checks and half-filled sheets
  • Follow-up calls and emails until the data is complete
.xlsx
missing

With WIN

One digital process, from request to import.

  • Requests go out straight from your ERP or the WIN web app
  • Producers enter data once, and WIN checks it while they type
  • Complete records arrive machine-to-machine, with no media breaks
  • You're notified the moment the data is ready to use

    Security

    Your data. Your rules.

    WIN isn't a data pool with open access. Producers decide who sees their data, and everything is hosted in Germany.

    Hosted in Germany

    All data sits on German servers and is covered by German and EU data protection law.

    No open pool

    Only merchants who send a request receive the product data. Nobody can browse it.

    Producer in control

    Producers release their data to individual merchants and keep control at all times.

    Full audit trail

    Every change is logged with user and time, so you can always prove where data came from.
